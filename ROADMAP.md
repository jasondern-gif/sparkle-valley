# Sparkle Valley Roadmap

The big idea: Sparkle Valley grows into a **pony exploration and rescue adventure**. Ponies are the main attraction. Farming is a tool she uses to help them, not the main gameplay.

Core loop: **Discover** a new pony → **Help** (complete a little quest) → **Reward** (a new friend) → **Unlock** a new place to explore.

Design rules (apply to everything):
- No grinding for coins, no long waits for crops, no failing quests, no punishment for taking a break.
- Every line is spoken out loud, with words lighting up as they are read. Every piece of text has a 🔊 button.
- Guiding arrow, big touch targets, automatic saving.
- Never wipe saved progress in an update.
- Not every interaction is a quest. She should be free to explore, pet ponies and play.
- Completing a quest leaves a visible, lasting change in her world.
